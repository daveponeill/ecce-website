The team

People

A small, collaborative group drawing on engineering, science education research, and twenty years of high school physics teaching.

Principal Investigator

David O'Neill, M.Eng., D.Phil. (he/him)

Professor of Instruction · Michael Jaharis Director of Experiential Learning · Associate Director, Northwestern Center for Engineering Education Research (NCEER)
Department of Biomedical Engineering, McCormick School of Engineering, Northwestern University

Read Engineering Science at Oxford (University College). M.Eng. 2008, D.Phil. 2012. Postdoctoral work in the Robbins Group (Dept. Physiology, Anatomy & Genetics, Oxford) — developed novel respiratory monitoring technology and computational models of blood gas transport. Joined Northwestern in 2018.

Research interests: engineering and research scientist identity, doctoral student wellbeing, and cultures of mentorship and assessment in engineering education.

Awards: Cole-Higgins Award for Excellence in Teaching (Northwestern, 2024); ASEE Biomedical Engineering Division (BED) Teaching Award (2023); Fletcher Prize for Excellence in Research Mentorship (Northwestern, 2022); RSC Horizon Prize: Sir George Stokes Award (2022).

Postdoctoral Fellow

Johan Tabora, Ph.D. (he/him)

Postdoctoral Research Fellow, Dept. BME, McCormick School of Engineering, Northwestern University

Research interests: the intersection of race, power, and STEM education; experiences of underrepresented Asian American populations in STEM.

Ph.D. Science Education, Dept. Curriculum & Instruction, University of Illinois Chicago. Ed.M. and M.S. Teacher Education, Harvard Graduate School of Education. B.S. Aerospace Engineering, Florida Institute of Technology. Before his postdoc, taught high school physics for 20 years in Chicago Public Schools.

At ECCE, Johan leads the longitudinal study of BME doctoral students' wellbeing and identity development through qualifying examinations.
